Questions owners ask

How long does PPF last?

Good film lasts for years when installed and cared for properly. We explain the warranty for the film we fit.

Ceramic coating or PPF, which is better?

PPF takes stone chips and scratches. Ceramic adds gloss and easy cleaning. Many owners fit both.

Do you make seat covers for every model?

Yes. We manufacture them ourselves, cut to your car's model.

How many days will my car stay?

Detailing can be same-day. PPF and full seat covers take longer. We confirm the time when you book.
